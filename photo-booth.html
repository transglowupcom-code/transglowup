🎨 WHAT YOU'RE GETTING

Nagel, in plain English: bold lines, flat colour, minimal shading, a very limited palette — black, white, alabaster skin, red lips — angular hair, figures cropped at the shoulders, and a simple geometric background. That's what the posterise + outline is chasing.

90s textiles: the Jazz cup pattern (1991, Gina Ekiss, Sweetheart/Solo) — teal + purple abstract swooshes, bold, asymmetric, playful. Memphis adds triangles, circles, squiggles and dots in hot pink, cyan, yellow and purple.

Darkwave + vaporwave UI: midnight background, neon pink + cyan glow, chrome sunset gradients, laser grids, CRT scanlines — clean, not cluttered. Controls are big arcade buttons, not tiny sliders hidden in a menu.

Sounds are synthesised live with Web Audio — an 80s shutter click + film whirr, and a chiptune arcade loop while your photo develops. No audio files, no copyrighted samples, no downloads, no cost.
